## 🌅 A Different Way to Look at Your Runs

PaceSmith is not another dashboard that spits out numbers and leaves you wondering what they mean. It is a **running intelligence studio** — a calm, thoughtful companion that reads the rhythm of every stride you have ever logged and translates it into a story about who you are becoming as a runner.

If you have ever finished a long Sunday run, looked at your watch, and felt that quiet question — *"Am I actually getting better?"* — PaceSmith was built for that exact moment.

Inspired by the raw energy of the open-source running analytics community, PaceSmith takes a completely different path. Instead of treating your activity history like a spreadsheet, it treats it like a **training diary written in the language of heartbeats, cadence, and elevation**. Every session becomes a paragraph. Every week becomes a chapter. Every season becomes an arc.

> "The clock tells you how fast you went. PaceSmith tells you how far you have come."

---

## 🧭 What Makes PaceSmith Distinct

Many tools stop at "here is your pace." PaceSmith goes further, asking deeper questions:

- How has your **aerobic efficiency** drifted over the last ninety days?
- Which weekday produces your most consistent efforts?
- Are your **easy runs actually easy**, or are you quietly overcooking them?
- When did your **cadence** quietly improve without you noticing?

This repository houses the entire engine — the analytics core, the web studio, the mobile bridge, and the documentation suite that keeps everything coherent.

## ✨ Feature Constellation

### 📊 Analytics Core
- **Trend Illumination** — long-horizon views across 30, 90, 180, and 365-day windows.
- **Pace Drift Detection** — notice subtle slowdowns before they become habits.
- **Heart Rate Zone Mapping** — understand where your effort really lives.
- **Cadence & Stride Insights** — biomechanics without the jargon.
- **Elevation Storytelling** — hills reframed as training partners, not obstacles.
- **Recovery Rhythm** — spot overreaching patterns across rolling weeks.
- **Personal Record Ledger** — distance, duration, and pace achievements tracked with grace.

### 🎨 Responsive User Interface
- Layouts that breathe on ultra-wide monitors and remain readable on a phone at dawn.
- A **dark pool** theme for late-night review sessions and a **sunrise** theme for morning planning.
- Keyboard-first navigation for the runners who think in shortcuts.
- Adaptive charts that scale from a smartwatch screen to a wall-mounted display.

### 🌍 Multilingual Support
- Interface available in **twelve languages**, with community-contributed translations.
- Locale-aware date, distance, and pace formatting.
- Right-to-left layout support for Arabic and Hebrew.
- Continuous translation pipeline so new strings arrive without waiting for a release cycle.

### 🛎️ 24/7 Customer Support
- Round-the-clock assistance for setup tribulations and analytics questions.
- A knowledge garden that grows with every question asked.
- Escalation paths that never leave a runner stranded on race week.
- Community forums moderated by experienced distance athletes.

### 🔄 Data Flow
- Connector modules that ingest activity exports from popular platforms.
- Incremental sync that respects rate limits and privacy boundaries.
- Local-first storage option for runners who prefer their data to stay home.
- Deterministic import pipeline — the same file always produces the same result.

### 🧪 Quality & Reliability
- Extensive unit and integration test suites.
- Snapshot tests for every chart component.
- Fuzz-tested parsers for messy activity files.
- Continuous benchmark tracking for analytics performance regressions.

### 🔐 Privacy Posture
- No shadow telemetry.
- Explicit consent for every remote call.
- Data export in open formats at any time.
- Self-hosting documentation for the privacy-conscious.

## 🧑‍💻 Getting Started Without the Usual Rituals

We deliberately avoid the standard installation incantations. Instead, here is the philosophy:

1. **Acquire the source via your preferred method** — the repository is yours to mirror, fork, or archive.
2. **Provision your runtime** using the version manifest found in the tooling directory.
3. **Bootstrap the workspace** with the bundled orchestration script that detects your environment.
4. **Launch the studio** and connect your first activity source.
5. **Invite a friend** — analytics are more fun when shared over coffee after a long run.

Detailed environment guidance lives in the documentation folder, written in plain prose rather than cryptic command lists.
